WebXR Classes

Mixed reality lessons that run in the browser — open them on a Meta Quest (or another WebXR headset), or explore on any screen.

Chemistry

Atom Scale Lab

Build and explore atomic models, compare the sizes of atoms and molecules, and discover the real scale of an atom — all you need to do is look out of the window.

 Computer Science

PC Builder Lab

Build your own computer from real, life-size parts like Lego — check that everything is compatible, power it on, and follow the power and data to see how the components work together.

 Biology

Cell Explorer

Investigate animal, plant and bacterial cells thousands of times bigger than life — name every part, pull organelles out to examine them, and see what happens to the cell when one goes missing.
