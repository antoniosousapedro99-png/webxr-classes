# Credits

Everything in the Kinetics Lab is made in code for this project: the bench, flasks, magnesium and marble, timers,
gas syringe, catalyst dishes and the class board. There are no third-party models, images or sounds, so there is
nothing to licence or credit beyond the project's own licence. three.js (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr, as in
every lesson.

The set-up follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson (flasks of acid at 20-40 degrees C, magnesium
strips dropped in and timed until they disappear, a timer with play/pause/reset), extended to the four factors of
the lesson plan.

The numbers are illustrative but follow the standard school patterns: the magnesium reaction roughly doubles in
rate for every 10 degrees C (40 s at 20 degrees C to 10 s at 40 degrees C); powdered marble reacts much faster than
chips or a lump; the gas volume follows V = 60 (1 - e^(-kt)) cm3 with k proportional to acid concentration;
hydrogen peroxide hardly decomposes without a catalyst, faster with copper(II) oxide and very fast with
manganese(IV) oxide. Each run varies by a few per cent.
