# Credits

Everything in the Enzyme Lab is made in code for this project: the bench, liver, scalpel, flask, hot plate,
stopper and delivery tube, trough and measuring cylinder, the bubbles and the class board. There are no
third-party models, images or sounds, so there is nothing to licence or credit beyond the project's own licence.
three.js (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr, as in every lesson.

The set-up follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson (cut the liver, put half in the flask, put
the stopper on, watch the water level in the cylinder).

The reaction: the share of hydrogen peroxide used up, x, grows as dx/dt = k (1 - x). k roughly doubles for each
10 degrees C and falls away as catalase denatures above about 45 degrees C, giving an optimum near 40 degrees C
and almost no oxygen at 70 degrees C and above. Each run varies by a few per cent, like real runs. Temperatures
change instantly (as if the flask had already been warmed in a water bath).
