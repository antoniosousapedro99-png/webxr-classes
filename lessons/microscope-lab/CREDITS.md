# Credits

Everything in the Microscope Lab is made in code for this project: the bench, slides, pipette, coverslips, the
microscope, every sample seen through the eyepiece (drawn in code, not photographs) and the class board. There
are no third-party models, images or sounds, so there is nothing to licence or credit beyond the project's own
licence. three.js (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr, as in every lesson.

The set-up follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson (pipette a drop onto a slide, coverslip on,
slide under the microscope, focus with - and +).

Sizes are realistic: fields of view of 4 mm, 1.6 mm and 0.4 mm for the x4, x10 and x40 lenses; onion cells about
300 x 70 micrometres, cheek cells about 60, pondweed cells 110 x 35, red blood cells 7.5, paramecia 120.
