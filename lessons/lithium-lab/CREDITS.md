# Credits

Everything in the Lithium Reactions Lab is made in code for this project: the bench, the metal cubes, the Bunsen
burner, the water tank, the safety screen, the reaction effects and sounds, and the class board. There are no
third-party models, images or sounds, so there is nothing to licence or credit beyond the project's own licence.
three.js (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr, as in every lesson.

The set-up follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson (pick up a metal cube, place it in the water
and observe; pick up a cube, place it over the fire and observe; a flame-colour poster and a reaction-with-water
reference chart).

The behaviour follows standard textbook descriptions: lithium, sodium and potassium float (densities 0.53, 0.97
and 0.89 g/cm3), rubidium and caesium sink (1.53 and 1.93 g/cm3); reaction speed and violence increase down the
group; flame colours are crimson (Li), orange-yellow (Na), lilac (K), red-violet (Rb) and blue (Cs). Reaction
times are illustrative, chosen to show the trend clearly, not measured values.
