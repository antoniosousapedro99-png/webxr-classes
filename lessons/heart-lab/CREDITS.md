# Credits

Everything in the Heart Lab is made in code for this project: the 3D heart (`src/model/Heart.js`), its muscle
texture (drawn on a canvas), the blood flow, the ECG and the heart sounds (synthesised with the Web Audio API).
There are no third-party models, images or sounds, so there is nothing to licence or credit beyond the project's
own licence. three.js (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr, as in every lesson.

Medical content (timings of the cardiac cycle, pressures, heart rates and stroke volumes) uses standard textbook
values for a healthy adult at rest, simplified for teaching.
